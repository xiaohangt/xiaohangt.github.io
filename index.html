Xiaohang Tang

Hi, and welcome — I'm Xiaohang. I'm currently a Research Scientist Intern at Google DeepMind, and a PhD candidate at UCL advised by Ilija Bogunovic. I have also been fortunate to work closely with Yaodong Yang. Before the PhD, I obtained my master's degree in Data Science at UCL and a bachelor's degree in Math at Sun Yat-sen University (SYSU). My research aims to build self-improving systems — agents and models for decision-making, reasoning, and generation — adapting to a wide range of important problems, from multi-agent systems and robust control to LLM alignment and post-training.
